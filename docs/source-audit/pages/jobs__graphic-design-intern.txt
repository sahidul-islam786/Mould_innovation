About the Role
We are looking for a highly motivated and creative Graphic Design Intern to join our team. As a Graphic Design Intern, you will work closely with our team of experienced designers to create visually stunning designs for a wide range of projects, including branding, print materials, digital media, and more. This is an excellent opportunity for someone who is passionate about design and eager to gain hands-on experience in a fast-paced agency environment.
We value creativity and innovation, and we offer a fun and energetic work environment where you can learn, grow, and make a real impact. If you're passionate about design and eager to join a dynamic team, we want to hear from you. Apply now!
[H2]
Requirements
[H3] KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:
Assist in the creation of visual design concepts and layouts for various projects
Work with the design team to develop and execute creative ideas
Assist in the production of print materials, such as brochures, posters, and packaging
Create graphics and layouts for digital media, such as social media posts, websites, and email campaigns
Collaborate with other teams, such as marketing and web development, to ensure that designs align with overall brand strategy
Help maintain design assets and project files
Continuously improve design skills and knowledge
[H3] QUALIFICATIONS:
Pursuing a degree in graphic design, visual communication, or a related field
Strong understanding of design principles and typography
Proficient in Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign)
Strong portfolio showcasing design skills
Understanding of digital media and web design is a plus
Excellent attention to detail and ability to work in a fast-paced environment
Strong communication and collaboration skills
Available to work a minimum of 20 hours per week
This is a part-time unpaid intern position with the possibility of conversion to a full-time position based on performance.
